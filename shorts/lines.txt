Stop scrolling!
Your phone might be stealing years of your life...
and you don't even realize it.
You open YouTube for just five minutes...
then one video becomes ten...
and suddenly, an entire hour is gone.
Here's the scary part:
every time you get a new notification,
your brain gets a reason to check your phone again.
And that tiny distraction can turn into a habit.
Try this today:
turn off unnecessary notifications,
and check your screen time.
You might be shocked by what you find.
Now be honest...
did you open YouTube to watch one video?
